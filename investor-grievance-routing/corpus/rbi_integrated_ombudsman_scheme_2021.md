## [RBI-IOS/Cl-3] Regulated entities covered by the Scheme
<!-- kind=scope; covers=bank,commercial bank,regional rural bank,cooperative bank,nbfc,payment system participant,credit information company,fixed deposit,savings account,loan,upi,debit card,credit card,net banking -->
The Scheme covers Regulated Entities, namely all commercial banks, regional rural banks, scheduled primary urban co-operative banks, non-banking financial companies which accept deposits or have an asset size of one hundred crore rupees or more, system participants under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, and credit information companies. A complaint against a bank about any banking service, including deposit accounts such as savings and fixed deposits, loans, cards and digital payments, falls within the Scheme.

## [RBI-IOS/Cl-8] Grounds of complaint — deficiency in service
<!-- kind=scope; covers=bank,nbfc,deficiency in service,fixed deposit,term deposit,interest,charges,payment failure -->
Any customer aggrieved by an act or omission of a Regulated Entity resulting in deficiency in service may file a complaint under the Scheme. Deficiency in service means a shortcoming or an inadequacy in any financial service which the Regulated Entity is required to provide, statutorily or otherwise, including failure to pay interest on a deposit at the rate agreed, levy of charges without notice, and failure or delay in crediting a payment.

## [RBI-IOS/Cl-10] Grounds for non-maintainability of a complaint
<!-- kind=exclusion; excludes=not first approached,court,sub judice,arbitration,time barred,commercial judgment; modifies=RBI-IOS/Cl-8 -->
No complaint for deficiency in service shall lie under the Scheme where the complainant has not first made a written complaint to the Regulated Entity, or where the Regulated Entity has not been given thirty days to reply; where the complaint is made more than one year after the complainant received the reply of the Regulated Entity; where the subject matter is pending before or decided by a court, tribunal, arbitrator or any other forum; or where the complaint concerns the commercial judgment of the Regulated Entity.

## [RBI-IOS/Cl-11] Procedure for filing a complaint
<!-- kind=procedure; covers=bank,nbfc,complaint management system -->
A complaint under the Scheme may be lodged online through the portal designated by the Reserve Bank for the purpose (the Complaint Management System), by email, or in physical form to the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre. The complaint is then handled by the Office of the RBI Ombudsman, which shall endeavour to promote a settlement between the complainant and the Regulated Entity.

## [RBI-IOS/FAQ-Deposits] Deposits held with a bank are banking services
<!-- kind=scope; covers=bank,fixed deposit,term deposit,recurring deposit,interest rate,mobile banking -->
Deposit accounts maintained with a bank, including term deposits opened at a branch or through internet or mobile banking, are banking services provided by a Regulated Entity. A grievance about such a deposit, including the interest applied to it, is taken up first with the bank and then with the RBI Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
