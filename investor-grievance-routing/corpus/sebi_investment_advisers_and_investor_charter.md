## [SEBI-IA/Reg-15] Investment adviser not to promise assured returns
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=investment adviser,assured returns,promised returns,mis-selling -->
An investment adviser shall not guarantee or promise any assured return to a client, either directly or indirectly, and shall not make any statement that is misleading as to the returns from an investment. A client who was promised returns by a registered investment adviser and received lower returns may complain against the investment adviser on SCORES.

## [SEBI-CHARTER/Rights] Investor Charter — rights of investors in the securities market
<!-- kind=scope; covers=investor,securities market,complaint,sebi,invested -->
Investors who have invested in the securities market have the right to receive services from SEBI-registered intermediaries and listed companies as promised, to be informed of the interest and charges applicable, and to get their complaints redressed in a time-bound manner. An investor who wishes to complain to SEBI about a listed company or a registered intermediary may lodge the complaint on SCORES.

## [SEBI-CHARTER/Dont] Investor Charter — what investors should avoid
<!-- kind=procedure; covers=investor,assured returns,unregistered entity -->
Investors should not deal with unregistered entities, should not fall for promises of assured or guaranteed returns, and should check that the entity they invest through is registered with SEBI before investing.
