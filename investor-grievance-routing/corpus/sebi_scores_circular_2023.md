## [SEBI-SCORES/2] Applicability of the grievance redressal framework
<!-- kind=scope; covers=listed company,stock broker,depository participant,depository,stock exchange,registrar and share transfer agent,mutual fund,asset management company,investment adviser,portfolio manager,research analyst -->
This framework applies to complaints lodged by investors against listed companies, registered intermediaries and market infrastructure institutions in the securities market. Registered intermediaries include stock brokers, depository participants, registrars to an issue and share transfer agents, mutual funds and their asset management companies, investment advisers, research analysts and portfolio managers. Market infrastructure institutions include stock exchanges, depositories and clearing corporations. Complaints are lodged on the SCORES platform, which is the centralised web-based complaint redress system of SEBI.

## [SEBI-SCORES/3] Investor to first approach the entity
<!-- kind=procedure; covers=listed company,stock broker,depository participant,mutual fund,registrar and share transfer agent -->
An investor shall first take up the grievance with the concerned listed company or registered intermediary. If the grievance is not redressed, or the investor is not satisfied with the response, the investor may lodge a complaint on SCORES. A complaint may be lodged on SCORES within three years from the date of the cause of action.

## [SEBI-SCORES/4] Timeline for the entity to resolve the complaint
<!-- kind=timeline; covers=listed company,stock broker,depository participant,mutual fund,asset management company,registrar and share transfer agent,investment adviser -->
A complaint lodged on SCORES is forwarded to the concerned entity, which shall resolve the complaint and upload an action taken report within 21 calendar days from the date of receipt of the complaint. The entity shall keep the complainant duly informed of the action taken.

## [SEBI-SCORES/5] First review by the Designated Body
<!-- kind=procedure; covers=stock broker,depository participant,listed company -->
Where the investor is not satisfied with the resolution provided by the entity, the investor may seek a first review by the Designated Body within 15 calendar days. The Designated Body shall complete the review within 10 calendar days. For complaints against stock brokers the Designated Body is the stock exchange where the broker is a member; for complaints against depository participants it is the depository; for complaints against listed companies it is the stock exchange where the securities are listed. A second review may be sought from SEBI.

## [SEBI-SCORES/6] Linkage to Online Dispute Resolution
<!-- kind=procedure; covers=stock broker,depository participant,mutual fund,listed company -->
If the complaint is not resolved through SCORES, the investor may initiate dispute resolution through the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal in accordance with the SEBI Master Circular on Online Resolution of Disputes in the Indian Securities Market.

## [SEBI-SCORES/Annex-A] Matters which are not dealt with as complaints on SCORES
<!-- kind=exclusion; excludes=bank,fixed deposit,term deposit,bank deposit,savings account,loan,upi,payment failure,insurance,pension,nps,chit fund,unlisted company,sub judice,court,query,suggestion,anonymous,crypto,cryptocurrency; modifies=SEBI-SCORES/2,SEBI-SCORES/3 -->
The following matters are not dealt with as complaints under SCORES:
(a) complaints that are incomplete, anonymous, or do not specify the entity complained against;
(b) suggestions, queries or requests for information, which are not grievances;
(c) matters which are sub judice, that is, pending before any court, tribunal, arbitrator or consumer forum;
(d) complaints against companies which are not listed, including companies which have been delisted, except for matters relating to the period of listing;
(e) complaints about products or services that fall under the purview of other regulators — for example complaints against banks about banking products such as bank deposits including fixed deposits, savings accounts, loans and payment failures, which fall under the Reserve Bank of India; insurance products, which fall under the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India; and pension products including the National Pension System, which fall under the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority. Such complaints should be lodged with the concerned regulator;
(f) complaints about products which are not regulated by SEBI, such as chit funds or crypto assets.
