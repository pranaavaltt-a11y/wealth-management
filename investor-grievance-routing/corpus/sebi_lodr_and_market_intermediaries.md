## [SEBI-LODR/Reg-13] Grievance redressal mechanism of listed entities
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=listed company,dividend,bonus,rights issue,annual report,share certificate -->
A listed entity shall ensure that adequate steps are taken for expeditious redressal of investor complaints, including complaints regarding non-receipt of dividend, bonus shares, rights entitlements, annual reports and share certificates. The listed entity shall be registered on SCORES and shall file with the recognised stock exchange, within twenty-one days from the end of each quarter, a statement of investor complaints received, disposed of and pending.

## [SEBI-BROKER/Grievance] Stock broker obligations towards clients
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=stock broker,trading,contract note,margin,demat,funds payout,unauthorised trade -->
A stock broker shall resolve complaints of clients relating to unauthorised trades, non-issue of contract notes, delay in payout of funds or securities, and wrong margin calls. The stock broker shall have a designated email address for investor grievances. Complaints against a stock broker are monitored by the stock exchange of which the broker is a member.

## [SEBI-DP/Grievance] Depository participant obligations
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=depository participant,depository,demat account,dematerialisation,transfer of securities,pledge -->
A depository participant shall redress complaints of beneficial owners relating to demat accounts, including delay in dematerialisation, unauthorised debit of securities from the demat account, and errors in pledge or transfer instructions. Complaints against a depository participant are monitored by the depository, namely NSDL or CDSL, with which the participant is registered.

## [SEBI-RTA/Grievance] Registrar and share transfer agent obligations
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=registrar and share transfer agent,transmission,transfer of physical shares,duplicate certificate,name change -->
A registrar and share transfer agent shall process investor service requests such as transmission of securities, issue of duplicate share certificates, change of name or address and consolidation of folios within the timelines specified by SEBI, and shall redress investor complaints relating to these requests. Where the registrar acts for a listed company, the listed company remains responsible to the investor.
