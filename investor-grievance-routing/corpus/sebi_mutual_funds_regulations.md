## [SEBI-MF-REGS/Reg-25] Asset management company responsible for investor servicing
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=mutual fund,asset management company,registrar and share transfer agent,sip,redemption,nav,idcw,folio -->
The asset management company of a mutual fund is responsible for the servicing of unit holders of its schemes. Where the asset management company appoints a registrar and transfer agent to maintain unit holder records and process transactions, the asset management company remains responsible to unit holders for the acts and omissions of the registrar. Complaints about mutual fund units, including systematic investment plans, redemptions, switches, NAV applied and income distribution, are complaints against the asset management company.

## [SEBI-MF-REGS/Reg-53] Timelines for redemption and income distribution
<!-- kind=timeline; covers=mutual fund,asset management company,redemption,idcw,dividend -->
Every mutual fund and asset management company shall dispatch redemption or repurchase proceeds to unit holders within the time specified by SEBI, which is three working days from the date of redemption, and shall dispatch income distribution (IDCW) proceeds within seven working days from the record date. In case of delay, the asset management company shall be liable to pay interest at the rate of fifteen per cent per annum for the period of delay.

## [SEBI-MF-REGS/Reg-18] Trustees to review investor grievances
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=mutual fund,trustee,asset management company -->
The trustees of a mutual fund shall periodically review the investor complaints received and the redressal of these complaints by the asset management company, and shall ensure that the asset management company addresses the grievances of unit holders.

## [SEBI-MF-REGS/Sch-Code] Code of conduct for distributors and mis-selling
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=mutual fund,distributor,asset management company,mis-selling -->
Mutual fund distributors shall not rebate commission, shall disclose all commissions payable to them, and shall not mis-sell schemes by making false or misleading statements about returns. Complaints about mis-selling of mutual fund units by a distributor are taken up with the asset management company whose scheme was sold.

## [SEBI-MF-REGS/STD] Parking of funds in short-term deposits of banks
<!-- kind=obligation; covers=mutual fund,asset management company,bank deposit,fixed deposit -->
Pending deployment of funds of a scheme in terms of its investment objective, a mutual fund may park the funds of the scheme in short-term deposits of scheduled commercial banks. Such deposits shall be held in the name of the scheme, shall not exceed the limits specified by SEBI, and the interest earned on them accrues to the scheme and is reflected in its net asset value. The asset management company is accountable to investors for the returns of the scheme.
